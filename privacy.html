Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy covers Travis Nelson Mail and Travis Nelson Calendar, desktop applications built by Travis Nelson Studio that connect to a person's own Google account.

What the apps access

  • Mail reads, organizes, labels and sends Gmail messages, and reads and updates Google Contacts, on the signed-in user's instruction.
  • Calendar reads the user's calendar list and reads, creates, changes and deletes calendar events.

Where data goes

Each app runs entirely on the user's own computer. Google data is fetched directly from Google's APIs and stored only in a local cache on that computer. Nothing is sent to servers operated by Travis Nelson Studio, and no Google data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Sign-in tokens are stored locally on the user's computer and can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Limited Use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

Removing an account in the app, or deleting the app's folder, erases the local cache and tokens. Revoking access in the Google Account settings stops all further access.
